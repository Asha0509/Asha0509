## What I've built

<img src="assets/card-healthai.svg" width="100%" alt="HealthAI Triage, an AI that can never talk you out of an emergency. In plain words: describe your symptoms and get one of three answers, care at home, see a doctor today, or go to the emergency room, with reasons and sources; fixed safety rules check every case and the most cautious answer wins. Under the hood: tool-calling agent over hybrid RAG with provider failover; final level is the maximum of the red-flag rules, the agent and a conformal second opinion; a 60-case eval in CI shows rules alone catch 100% of red-flag cases and 85% of emergencies; 135 tests."/>

## Research

<img src="assets/card-research.svg" width="100%" alt="Swapping the Model Does Not Reset the Price, research with Apart, October 2026. In plain words: if AI agents start colluding on prices and you replace the model behind one of them, the problem does not go away; with the old agent's notes, the new model carried on exactly where the old one left off in 18 of 18 test markets. Under the hood: pre-registered replacement study; markets formed over 150 rounds, then one firm's model was swapped under notes, clean-slate and placebo conditions; with notes, prices settle in a median of 1 round, from a clean slate 16.5; scanning memory for collusive language would miss this channel."/>
